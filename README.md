# Hackathon: ideation and planning

Work through the files in order. Each question has a **Draft (fallback)** answer taken from the earlier plan. Keep it, change it or replace it. A fallback only stands if we don't choose something better.

- `docs/planning/00-event.md`: the event, the schedule and the judging criteria
1. `docs/planning/01-problem.md`: who has the problem and why it matters
2. `docs/planning/02-ideas.md`: options, then pick one
3. `docs/planning/03-users-and-demo.md`: who it's for and the moment a judge must see
4. `docs/planning/04-scope.md`: must, should, could, won't, and what to cut first
5. `docs/planning/05-ui-direction.md`: how it looks and feels
6. `docs/planning/06-build-and-delivery.md`: how and where it gets built and shown
7. `docs/planning/decisions.md`: every decision, with who made it and when

## Layout
- `docs/planning/`: planning files, `decisions.md` and `HANDOFF.md`
- `mockup/`: UI mockups and rendered screens (`mockup/screens/`)
- `kit/design/tokens.css`: design tokens the mockups use
