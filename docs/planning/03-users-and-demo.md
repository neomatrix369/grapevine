**Demo path (LOCKED 22 Oct 19:08, about 2 min):**
> 1. Land: one screen, coverage line showing ("based on N of M channels, since {date}").
> 2. Ask: type one real question into the compose box.
> 3. Already known: a matching thread and topic note appear (Library).
> 4. Experts: who to tag, each with proof (Experts).
> 5. Write: pick channels, the rules check flags a cross-post, tag one expert, copy and open (nothing is sent automatically).
> 6. My posts: the question appears as "awaiting replies".
> 7. Grow: read one more channel live; Growth counts go up and a new expert appears.
> 8. Announce: flip the switch, paste a hackathon announcement, see where it's allowed and whom to contact, pick three mentors.
> 9. End card, with the "how it was built" proof: `docs/BUILT-WITH.md` and tagged commits (added 19:33, Mani).

**What makes it believable?**
> **Decided 19:33 (Mani, kept fallback):** real data, proof links on every claim, an honest coverage line, and one channel read live on stage.
