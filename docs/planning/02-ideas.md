# 2. Ideas

**Chosen direction (draft): the best of everything discussed, as one product with five parts.**

| # | Part | What the user gets | Reuses from the earlier plan | Gap filled now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Knowledge library** | Topics and sub-topics, a short note per topic with its source threads, and a cheat sheet of the most-shared links | Topics tab, cheat sheet, evidence on every note, freshness labels | Search the library directly, not only from a question |
| 2 | **Experts directory** | Who the go-to people are per topic, with proof ("8 answers in #channel"), last active, and connection strength | People list and graph, evidence, fair tagging, active badge, hide a person | A browsable directory by topic and community |
| 3 | **Write once, post to the right channels** | Compose a question, see the best channels across communities, the rules that apply, and optionally tag suggested experts | Compose box, best channel, who to tag, rules guard and pre-post check, draft, copy-and-open | Pick several target channels; the guard warns on cross-posting; tags are opt-in per expert. Sending stays copy-and-open (no automated posting, to avoid account restrictions) |
| 4 | **My posts** | Everything you've asked: where, when, replies, answered or not, and who answered | Me panel (private) | New: a private "My posts" list built from what's been read plus posts started here, with status |
| 5 | **Watch the knowledge grow** | Coverage over time, "+N threads, +N topics, +N experts since last visit", a gap you can read live | Coverage heatmap, confidence line, backfill, "Read next" | New: a small growth view showing counts per week as the backfill lands |

**Organiser mode (event and hackathon organisers), the same five parts pointed at a new job:**

| # | Feature | What the organiser gets | Built on |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | **Announce once** | Paste an event or hackathon announcement and see which channels across communities allow events, which rule blocks a channel (quoted), whom to contact first (moderators and organisers), and the best time to post, then copy and open each channel | Part 3 (write once) plus the rules guard; the earlier announce-mode teaser |
| O2 | **Find mentors, judges and speakers** | Filter the experts directory by topic for an event's themes: the top people with proof, last active, and whether they've helped newcomers before | Part 2 (experts directory) |
| O3 | **Community pulse** | Recurring unanswered questions (ideas for talks, workshops or hackathon tracks), the busiest topics this month, and where the gaps are | Part 1 (library) plus part 5 (growth) |
| O4 | **Track announcements** | Each announcement listed with where it went, replies and reactions | Part 4 (my posts) |

Draft priority: O1 and O2 as Should (one short demo beat at the end), O3 as Could, O4 for free once My posts exists.
